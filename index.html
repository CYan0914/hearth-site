What's inside

A maintenance schedule, the history behind it, and the paperwork for everything you own — in one place.

A passport for every appliance

Brand, model, serial, location, warranty status and service history, attached to the thing they belong to. When a part fails, you already know whether the warranty covers it.

Reads the plate, not a checklist

Brand, model and serial come off the nameplate in one photo. Hearth looks up what that specific model actually needs — the interval the manufacturer specifies, not the one from a generic list taped inside a cupboard door.

Recalls, checked for you

Search any brand or model against the Consumer Product Safety Commission's recall database. Hearth also checks what you already own, every night, and tells you when one turns up.

Keep the proof

Log what you did, what it cost and who did it. Photos of nameplates, receipts and serials stay with the appliance. Useful when you sell the house, file a claim, or argue about a warranty.

Private by default

Sign in with Apple. No adverts, no trackers, no data brokers. Deleting your account removes everything, photos included, in one step.
